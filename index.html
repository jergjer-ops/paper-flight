How to play

  • Tap the screen — the plane gains height
  • Fly between the pipes: each pipe = 1 point
  • Every 5 pipes — bonus: +5 coins and a parachute
  • Catch the parachute — it gives +1 life (max 3)
  • A collision with a pipe, the ground or the ceiling costs a life
  • No lives left — game over
  • Ride the updrafts — they lift you up
